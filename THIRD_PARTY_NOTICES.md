# Third-party content

Six puzzles in `src/data/puzzles.ts` are adapted from Sarah Withee's
[puns repository](https://github.com/geekygirlsarah/puns), specifically
`technology.md` and `science.md`. Each adapted puzzle records its source.
Clues were adapted for interactive play; hints and accepted-answer variants
were added for this game. The original three puzzles are unchanged.

The upstream MIT license is preserved in `public/licenses/geekygirlsarah-puns.txt`
and is included in web exports at `/licenses/geekygirlsarah-puns.txt`.

This is a curated local integration, not a live feed. Review new entries for
suitability and answer quality before adding them. Append entries to preserve
the current index-based saves.
